## Rules for every shot (from the brief, plus past lessons)

- **Nobody talks to Alia, or looks at a phone, while operating a tool.** Tools are set down, switched off, or out of focus in the background.
- **The cap never replaces PPE.**
  - The lead wears the cap only when he isn't doing tool work.
  - Crew members working in the background wear what their task needs: gloves, safety glasses and hi-vis; helmet with ear defenders and a face screen for saw or brushcutter work.
  - Residential landscape jobs, trucks and a dealer counter only. **No building sites** where hard hats are required.
- **No brands.**
  - Tools are plain dark grey or black, with no orange-and-white body (that would read as STIHL product imagery, which we have no rights to).
  - Trucks are unbadged. Generated vehicles and tools often come back with real logos, so plan a delogo pass (ffmpeg delogo on the source, root first).
  - No readable signs or text.
- **Prompt style:** a shot sheet (camera, lens, one light source, small imperfections, subject at 35–50% of the frame). Never "hyperreal", "8K" or "stunning".
- **The cast is consistent:** Ryan, crew lead, early 40s, short beard, canvas work jacket, the greyer cap. Javier, crew, late 20s, hi-vis vest and work gloves. Karen Holt, homeowner, 50s.
- **One shared grade after selection:** lifted blacks, fine grain, faint vignette, no sharpening.

**P1 · Tailgate, safe pause (4:5).**
Documentary photo, 35mm lens at f/2.8, eye level, late-afternoon side light from the left, overcast sky.
- Ryan stands at the open tailgate of an unbadged dark pickup on a suburban street. He's mid-sentence, eyes down and to the side, one hand resting on the tailgate. He wears the greyer cap (images 1–4) and a tan canvas work jacket.
- A plain dark-grey gas trimmer lies switched off on the tailgate bed beside a pair of work gloves.
- Behind him, out of focus, two crew members in hi-vis and safety glasses rake leaves into a tarp. No engine running.
- He fills about 45% of the frame on the right third. Leave clean, darker space on the left for the gradient.
- Slight motion blur in the crew, a scuff on the tailgate, natural skin texture. No logos, no readable text.

- Everyone's tools are off. The crew wear gloves, safety glasses and hi-vis vests.
- Ryan holds a phone loosely at his side. The group fills about 40% of the frame, centred, with room around them.

- The two fill about 45% of the frame.
